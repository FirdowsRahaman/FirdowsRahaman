* **European Union & EMEA (EU AI Act & GDPR):**  
  Architecting AI platforms compliant with the **EU AI Act** across all risk tiers (prohibited, high-risk systems, and General Purpose AI models) and **GDPR**. Specializing in sovereign data residency (EU AWS/GCP regions), zero-retention data pipelines, automated algorithmic audit logs, model explainability, and private self-hosted inference.

* **Asia-Pacific & South Asia (APAC Markets & DPDP Act):**  
  Leading regional AI adoption tailored to national data protection mandates (such as India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act - DPDP** and APAC localization standards). Structuring sovereign hybrid-cloud deployments, local compute residency, and cross-border data transfer safeguards that respect regional regulatory policies.

* **United States (US Market — NIST AI RMF, HIPAA, SOC 2):**  
  Deploying enterprise AI aligned with the **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)**, **HIPAA** (for clinical & healthcare AI), and **SOC 2 Type II**. Engineering automated PII/PHI redaction, role-based access control (RBAC), prompt injection defenses, and zero-trust agent authorization layers.

| **Article 13: Transparency & Explainability** | RAG source attribution, citation verification, and confidence thresholding | Hallucination guardrails & verifiable source citation |
